# 🔴🟢 Simon: Official Rules & Acoustic Specifications

> *"Listen, remember, and repeat. As the pattern lengthens, the tempo accelerates."*

---

## 📖 1. Origin & Heritage
Invented by Ralph H. Baer and Howard J. Morrison and released by **Milton Bradley in 1978**, Simon is one of the most recognizable audio-visual memory games in electronic entertainment history.

---

## 🎯 2. Core Game Rules

### 2.1 The Console
- The device features four large illuminated quadrant lenses:
  - **Green** (Top-Left)
  - **Red** (Top-Right)
  - **Yellow** (Bottom-Left)
  - **Blue** (Bottom-Right)

### 2.2 Gameplay Loop
1. **Demonstration Phase**: The console illuminates a sequence of lenses one by one, each accompanied by its unique harmonic pitch.
2. **Player Phase**: The player must press the lenses in the exact identical sequence.
3. **Progression**:
   - Each round completed successfully increments the sequence by **1 new step**.
   - The playback speed ramps up at intervals (e.g. rounds 5, 9, 13).
4. **Game Over**:
   - In **Standard Mode**, an incorrect press plays the error buzzer and shows your final score.
   - In **Strict Mode**, any mistake immediately resets the round count back to zero.
