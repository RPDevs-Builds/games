# 🐸 Frogger (1981) - Game Rules & Strategy Guide

## Historical Background
Developed by **Konami** and released in June 1981 (licensed to Sega in North America), *Frogger* is one of the most beloved arcade games of the Golden Age. Frogger introduced the novel mechanic of navigating bidirectional multi-lane hazards without firing any weapons. The game challenges players with pure timing, spatial judgment, and pathfinding under a ticking clock.

---

## Game Rules & Mechanics

### 1. Goal & Structure
- The arena is divided into **14 horizontal rows**:
  - **Row 0: Goal Docks**: 5 open lilypad home bays separated by impassable bush barriers.
  - **Rows 1 to 5: River Rapids**: Fast logs, giant logs, and diving turtles moving across deadly water.
  - **Row 6: Purple Median Grass**: Safe resting sidewalk.
  - **Rows 7 to 11: Highway Traffic**: Racecars, tractors, sedans, delivery vans, and semi-trucks.
  - **Row 12: Starting Sidewalk**: Safe frog spawn line.
  - **Row 13: Timer & HUD Bar**: Shows remaining countdown time, level, and saved frogs.

### 2. Crossing the Highway (Rows 7 to 11)
- The frog must dodge oncoming traffic.
- Colliding with any vehicle instantly squashes the frog (losing 1 life).

### 3. Crossing the River (Rows 1 to 5)
- River water is deadly! The frog drowns if it hops into open water.
- The frog must land on and ride:
  - **Logs**: Always safe to ride.
  - **Turtles**: Safe while surfaced. Watch out for **diving turtles**! When their shells submerge underwater, any riding frog drowns.
- **Off-Screen Limit**: If a log or turtle carries the frog past the left or right edge of the arena, the frog is lost.

### 4. Reaching the Home Bays (Row 0)
- The frog must leap cleanly into one of the **5 empty goal bays**.
- Leaping into a bush wall or an already-occupied bay destroys the frog.
- When a frog lands safely:
  - Awards **50 base points**.
  - Awards **10 points per remaining second** on the timer.
  - If a **Bonus Fly** is buzzing in the bay, awards an extra **200 bonus points**!
- Filling all 5 home bays clears the round, awards **1,000 bonus points**, and advances to the next round with increased traffic and river speeds.

### 5. Time Limit & Lives
- Players start with **3 Frogs (Lives)**.
- Each frog has a **30-second time bar**. If the timer runs out before docking, the frog dies.
- Extra frog awarded at **10,000 points**.

| :--- | :---: |
