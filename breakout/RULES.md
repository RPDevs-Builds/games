# 🧱 Breakout (1976) - Rules & Strategy Guide

## Overview
**Breakout** is the quintessential arcade classic conceptualized by Nolan Bushnell and Steve Bristow, and built by Steve Wozniak at Atari in 1976. The player maneuvers a horizontal paddle at the base of the screen to ricochet a ball upward, breaking through a dense layer of multi-colored bricks.

---

## 🎮 How to Play

### Objectives
1. **Deflect the Ball**: Keep the ball in play by positioning your paddle under the ball's descent path.
2. **Smash Bricks**: Every brick struck disappears immediately and scores points.
3. **Clear the Screen**: Destroy all 64 bricks to achieve victory.
4. **Preserve Lives**: You start with 3 lives. If the ball bypasses your paddle and drops below the bottom boundary, one life is lost.

## ⚡ Master Strategies & Ball Deflection Physics

1. **Angular Deflection**:
   - Striking the ball at the **center of the paddle** returns it straight up with low horizontal drift.
   - Striking the ball on the **outer edges of the paddle** launches it at a steep deflection angle (up to $\pm 65^\circ$). Use sharp angles to reach narrow corners.

2. **The Breakthrough Tactic**:
   - Carve a vertical tunnel through one edge of the wall (preferably left or right).
   - Once the ball punches through into the space above the red bricks, it traps itself between the top ceiling and the back of the brick wall, automatically clearing dozens of bricks without risking bottom paddle loss.

## 🕹️ Controls
- **Touch / Mobile**: Drag your finger across the arena to smoothly move the paddle. Tap the arena or **Launch** button to release the ball.
- **Keyboard**:
  - `ArrowLeft` / `A`: Move paddle Left
  - `ArrowRight` / `D`: Move paddle Right
  - `Space` / `Enter`: Launch Ball
  - `R`: Restart Game
- **Gamepad**: Analog stick / D-Pad to move, (A) button to launch.
