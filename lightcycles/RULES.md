# Tron Light Cycles (1982) - Rules & Game Manual

## Overview
Tron Light Cycles is inspired by the iconic 1982 cyber arena combat game. You pilot a high-speed light cycle leaving an impenetrable solid neon light wall behind you. The objective is to outmaneuver opponents, cut off their escape paths, force them into collisions, and avoid touching walls or trails.

---

## Game Rules & Mechanics

1. **The Arena Grid**:
   - The battle takes place on a high-resolution cybernetic grid with boundary laser walls.
   - Any collision with boundary walls or light trails instantly de-rezes (destroys) the cycle.

2. **Movement & Trail**:
   - Cycles move forward constantly at uniform speed.
   - Turns are locked to 90-degree orthogonal directions (Up, Down, Left, Right).
   - Immediate 180-degree reversals are prohibited (you cannot steer directly into your own trailing light beam).
   - Solid trails persist until the round ends.

3. **Turbo Boost**:
   - Each player possesses a rechargeable boost capacitor.
   - Activating boost triples cycle speed, allowing aggressive intercept maneuvers or rapid escapes from closing pockets.
   - Boosting drains capacitor reserves; recharge occurs automatically over time when boost is disengaged.

4. **Game Modes**:
   - **Solo vs CPU**: Battle against adaptive flood-fill tactical AI across 3 difficulty tiers (Novice, Adept, Master).
   - **2-Player Pass & Play**: Local dual duel on the same screen (Keyboard P1: WASD / Space, P2: Arrow keys or IJKL / Enter).

---

## Controls

### Desktop / Keyboard
- **Player 1**:
  - Steer: `W` / `A` / `S` / `D` or `Arrow Keys`
  - Turbo Boost: `Space` or `Shift`
- **Player 2 (2-Player Mode)**:
  - Steer: `I` / `J` / `K` / `L` or `Numpad`
  - Turbo Boost: `Enter` or `Keypad 0`
- **General**:
  - Pause: `P` or `Escape`
  - Reset / Restart: `R`

### Mobile & Touch
- **On-Screen D-Pad**: Tap Up / Down / Left / Right arrows to steer.
- **Boost Button**: Hold `BOOST` button for high-velocity surge.
- **Direct Swiping**: Swipe in four directions on the arena canvas.

### Gamepad (XInput / Bluetooth Arcade Sticks)
- **D-Pad / Left Stick**: Steer light cycle.
- **A Button / Right Trigger**: Turbo boost.
- **Start**: Pause / Resume.

---

## Scoring & Achievements
- **Round Win**: +1000 base points.
- **Speed Win**: Bonus score proportional to remaining boost capacitor.
- **Survival Streak**: Multipliers rewarded for consecutive victories against CPU.
