🔤 WORDLE

HOW TO PLAY

Guess the WORDLE in 6 attempts.

Each guess must be a valid 5-letter word. Hit the ENTER button to submit.

After each guess, the color of the tiles will change to show how close your guess was to the word.

Game Modes

Daily Seed: Everyone gets the exact same word based on today's calendar date!

Practice Mode: Endless free play with unlimited randomized words.

Hard Mode: Any revealed green letter must be reused in the exact same position, and any yellow letter must appear in subsequent guesses.
