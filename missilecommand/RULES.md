# 🚀 Missile Command (1980) - Rules & Strategy Guide

## Overview
**Missile Command** is the landmark 1980 arcade defense classic designed by Dave Theurer and released by Atari. The player coordinates planetary anti-ballistic missile defense against incoming interplanetary ballistic warheads, MIRV cluster splits, and orbital bombers.

---

## 🎮 How to Play

### Objectives
1. **Defend 6 Cities**: Protect the six metropolitan clusters situated along the coastline.
2. **Manage 3 Missile Silos**:
   - **Alpha** (Left Silo): Defends Western corridor.
   - **Delta** (Center Silo): Rapid central deployment with high trajectory efficiency.
   - **Omega** (Right Silo): Defends Eastern corridor.
3. **Timed Flak Detonation**: Interceptors do not hit missiles directly; rather, they detonate at the crosshair coordinate, producing an expanding flak fireball that dissolves passing warheads.
4. **Survive Progressive Waves**: Each wave increases missile counts, descent velocity, and MIRV split frequency.

---

## 📐 Scoring & Wave Bonuses

- **Interception**: 25 points per warhead destroyed $\times$ Wave multiplier.
- **Surviving Cities**: 100 points per living city $\times$ Wave multiplier.
- **Unused Ammunition**: 5 points per remaining rocket $\times$ Wave multiplier.

---

## ⚡ Master Strategies

1. **Lead Your Targets**:
   - Interceptors require travel time from their silo to the designated target coordinate. Always aim ahead of descending warheads along their predicted trajectory.
2. **Chain Reaction Fireballs**:
   - A single well-placed detonation can catch multiple converging warheads or trigger chain explosions as warheads split.
3. **Conserve Delta (Center Silo)**:
   - Delta possesses the shortest flight time to the greatest area of the radar screen. Conserve its ammunition for emergency intercepts close to ground level.

---

## 🕹️ Controls
- **Mouse / Touch**: Aim crosshair anywhere on the radar screen. Left-click or tap to launch interceptor.
- **Keyboard**:
  - `A`: Fire from Alpha (Left) Silo
  - `S`: Fire from Delta (Center) Silo
  - `D`: Fire from Omega (Right) Silo
  - `Space`: Smart Fire from closest active silo
- **Gamepad**: Analog stick to steer crosshair, `(X)`, `(A)`, `(B)` buttons for left, center, right silos.
